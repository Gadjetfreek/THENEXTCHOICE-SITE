A question for this moment

Are you trying to be understood — or understand?

▶ Hear the story

There is no right answer. Just give the question a little room — or hear the story and tell us what you noticed.

The Next Choice explores a simple idea: what if technology gave us more time to think, notice, listen, and make better choices — instead of always asking for more of our attention?

We imagine a future where we get better at solving problems together, understanding one another, and caring for the living world we are part of.

We do not know exactly what that future will look like. That is the point. We are still making it.

The Human Story is not even close to finished.
