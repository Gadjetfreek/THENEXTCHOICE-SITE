Our Future Vision starts with The Next Choice.

A question for this moment

What should we do with what we are becoming capable of?

There is no answer to submit. Give the question some room. Notice what changes when you consider the people, other life, and future generations who will live with the consequences.

The Next Choice asks what happens when greater capability is matched by greater human agency, wisdom, relationship, and responsibility.

Technology can help us see more, understand consequences earlier, reduce unnecessary burdens, and widen the choices we can perceive. It should not quietly decide what humanity ought to become.

We are not separate from life. We are participants in it. The future is being built continuously through choices made by people, communities, institutions, technologies, and increasingly capable forms of intelligence.

The earlier Human Trial remains part of the experiment.

Let’s make the future we are building together one worth being alive in.

The Human Story is not even close to finished.
