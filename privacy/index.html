The Next Choice

Privacy

Short version: if you choose to answer one of our questions, we use your response to understand how people think about these ideas and to improve The Next Choice. Your first name is optional. We do not post responses publicly or sell them.

What we collect

We collect the answers you choose to submit and, only if you provide it, your first name. While you are answering, your in-progress responses may also be stored temporarily in your browser tab so that Back, Forward, or a refresh does not erase your work.

Why we collect it

We use responses to learn whether our questions and ideas are understandable, useful, and worth continuing to explore. We may review responses individually or in aggregate to improve the project.

Where submitted responses go

Submitted responses are sent through a Google Apps Script collector into a private project workspace controlled by The Next Choice team. The public website does not display the submitted answers.

What we do not do

We do not sell submitted responses. We do not intentionally publish a respondent's answer or first name without separate permission. We do not require a name in order to participate.

Please avoid sensitive information

The questions are intended for reflection, not for collecting confidential, medical, financial, legal, account, password, or other highly sensitive information. Please do not include information you would not want the project team to read.

Retention and deletion

We keep submitted responses only as long as they remain useful for the project's research, development, and recordkeeping needs. If a deletion request can reasonably identify a response, we will make a good-faith effort to remove it from our active records.

Children

The response form is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such personal information was submitted, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

Questions or deletion requests

Until a dedicated privacy contact is published, contact The Next Choice through the same official channel through which you reached the project and identify the response as clearly as you reasonably can.
